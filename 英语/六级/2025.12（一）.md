### 逐句批改与分析：

**1.** _While striving for the Chinese Dream, young people enjoy more opportunities to realize their self-worth. They merge their own purpose into Chinese Dream._

- **批改：** 抄写原句很好，引出话题。但第二句缺乏定冠词，且 _purpose_ (目的) 用单数不够贴切，建议改为 _goals_ (目标) 或 _ambitions_。
- **修正：** They are merging their personal **goals** into **the** Chinese Dream.

**2.** _Recently, our country has fast development and become one of the most powerful country in the word._

- **批改：** 语法错误频出。_has_ 后应接名词，但表达“发展快”通常用动词 _has developed rapidly_ 或 _has experienced rapid development_。_one of the..._ 后面必须接**复数名词** (_countries_)。_word_ (单词) 拼写错误，应为 _world_ (世界)。
- **修正：** In recent years, our country has **experienced rapid development** and has become one of the most powerful **countries** in the **world**.

**3.** _But, we still face lots of problem, such as low-income._

- **批改：** _problem_ 必须用复数 _problems_。_low-income_ 作为名词短语不够完整，可以说 _income inequality_（收入不均）或 _economic challenges_。
- **修正：** However, we still face many **problems**, such as economic challenges.

**4.** _So, we can't immerse into the success we got._

- **批改：** _immerse into_ 通常指“沉浸在（好的事物中，如学习、阅读）”，带有积极色彩。这里你想表达“沉溺于/沾沾自喜”，用 _be complacent about_ 或 _rest on our laurels_（躺在功劳簿上）更高级。
- **修正：** Therefore, we cannot **be complacent about** the success we have achieved.

**5.** _Young people not only need to keep going, but also to combine their self-worth with Chinese Dream._

- **批改：** 同样缺少定冠词 _the_。_keep going_ 过于口语化，可以提升为 _forge ahead_ (奋勇向前)。
- **修正：** Young people not only need to **forge ahead**, but also combine their self-worth with **the** Chinese Dream.

**6.** _They can devote themselves into new technology to break the block of technology._

- **批改：** _devote oneself to_ 后面接动名词（_to_ 是介词）。“打破技术封锁/壁垒”通常用 _break through technical bottlenecks_ 或 _technological barriers_，用 _block of technology_ 太中式。
- **修正：** They can devote themselves **to developing** new technologies to **break through technological barriers**.

**7.** _Each of us borned with the unbelievable development of China, we've enjoyed the flourished economy._

- **批改：** 严重语法错误。_born_ 本身已经是动词的过去分词，不存在 _borned_ 这个词。逗号连接了两个完整句子，导致 Run-on sentence（流水句）。_flourished_ (已经繁荣过的) 不如用现在分词 _flourishing_ (正在繁荣的)。
- **修正：** **Having been born during** the unbelievable development of China, we have enjoyed a **flourishing** economy.

**8.** _So, today, it's our duty to strive for the Chinese Dream._

- **批改：** 句子正确，但 _So_ 在书面语中建议替换为 _Therefore_ 或 _Thus_。

**9.** _Part of young people throught it's too huge and they can't do it because of their small power._

- **批改：** 拼写错误：_throught_ 应为 _thought_。“一部分年轻人”用 _Some young people_ 更自然。“力量渺小”不要直译为 _small power_，用 _feel powerless_ (感到无力) 更好。
- **修正：** **Some** young people **think** the dream is too grand and feel **powerless to contribute**.

**10.** _It is each young people a country is developed by._

- **批改：** 典型的中式倒装句，语法完全不通。且 _young people_ 是复数，不能用 _each_ (应为 _each young person_)。
- **修正：** **However, the development of a country relies on every individual youth.**

**11.** _In total, the realize of Chinese should be done by young people._

- **批改：** “总而言之”通常用 _In conclusion_ 或 _To sum up_，_In total_ 表示“总计/合计”（多用于数学或数量）。_realize_ 是动词，这里需要名词 _realization_。“中华民族的伟大复兴”不要简写成 _Chinese_。
- **修正：** **In conclusion, the realization of the Chinese Dream depends on the hard work of our young generation.**
